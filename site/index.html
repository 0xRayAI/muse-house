How it works

Three steps. No setup screens from 2009.

1

Connect your apps

You answer a few questions about your life, and it suggests which apps to connect — bank, inbox, calendar. Read-only where it matters. You approve everything.

2

Your house gets built

A short conversation becomes your house: nine rooms for Art, Bling, Coach, Dev, Game, Health, Home, Money, and Travel, each with its own space, routines, and rules about what it can do on its own.

3

It runs every day

Morning briefing. Bill watch. Evening wrap. Your rooms live as chats inside Muse — ask anything, approve what matters, ignore the rest.

House rules

Helpful, never reckless.

Every house runs on a Codex — plain rules the house cannot break:

✓

Confirm before it sends, spends, shares, or deletes. Anything irreversible waits for you.

✓

Verify before it claims. Balances, dates, and prices come from real sources, not guesses.

✓

Your information stays yours. Nothing you tell your house is shared, published, or sold.

Privacy

Your data never leaves your house.

Nothing stored

The Muse House service keeps nothing about you. It hands your Muse blueprints and answers questions — your information lives with your assistant, not on our servers.

Read-only money

Bank connections see balances and transactions. They cannot move a cent. Spending always needs your explicit approval.

Open source

The whole service is public on GitHub. Read the code, run it yourself, verify the privacy claims.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Muse House?
A personal operating system inside Muse. Nine dedicated rooms — Art, Bling, Coach, Dev, Game, Health, Home, Money, and Travel — each with its own board, daily routines, and rules about what it may do without asking.

How does it work?
Three steps: connect your apps (it suggests which ones to turn on), answer a few questions to build your house, and it runs daily — morning briefing, bill watch, evening wrap.

Does it store my data?
No. Nothing about you is kept on our servers. It hands your Muse blueprints and answers questions — your information lives with your assistant, not with us.

Can it move my money or pay bills?
Never. Bank connections are read-only. Every house runs on a Codex that requires your confirmation before anything is sent, spent, shared, or deleted.

Is it in the Muse connector directory?
Directory listing is draft / not submitted. You don't have to wait — add it as a custom connector today (see "Move in today" below) and it works the same.

Is it free?
Yes. The blueprints are free and the service is open source. Connect it as a custom connector in Muse and set up your house in minutes.
