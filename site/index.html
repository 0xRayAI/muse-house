How it works

Three steps. No setup screens from 2009.

1

Connect your utilities

The Foundry looks at your life and suggests which Muse connectors to turn on — bank, inbox, calendar. Read-only where it matters. You approve everything.

2

The Foundry builds your house

A short conversation becomes your House: rooms for Money, Travel, Home, and Health, each with its own board, routines, and rules about what it may do alone.

3

The Mill runs it daily

Morning briefing. Bill watch. Evening wrap. Your rooms live as chats inside Muse — ask anything, approve what matters, ignore the rest.

House rules

Helpful, never reckless.

Every house runs on a Codex — plain rules the house cannot break:

✓

Confirm before it sends, spends, shares, or deletes. Anything irreversible waits for you.

✓

Verify before it claims. Balances, dates, and prices come from real sources, not guesses.

✓

Your information stays yours. Nothing you tell your house is shared, published, or sold.

Privacy

Your data never leaves your house.

Stateless by design

The Muse House service stores nothing about you. It hands your Muse blueprints and answers questions — your board lives with your assistant, not on our servers.

Read-only money

Bank connections see balances and transactions. They cannot move a cent. Spending always needs your explicit approval.

Open source

The whole service is public on GitHub. Read the code, run it yourself, verify the privacy claims.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Muse House?
A personal operating system inside Muse. Dedicated rooms for Money, Travel, Home, Health, Game, Art, and Dev — each with its own board, daily routines, and rules about what it may do without asking.

How does it work?
Three steps: connect your utilities (the Foundry suggests which Muse connectors to turn on), the Foundry builds your house from a short conversation, and the Mill runs it daily — morning briefing, bill watch, evening wrap.

Does it store my data?
No. The service is stateless by design. It hands your Muse blueprints and answers questions — your board lives with your assistant, not on our servers.

Can it move my money or pay bills?
Never. Bank connections are read-only. Every house runs on a Codex that requires your confirmation before anything is sent, spent, shared, or deleted.

Is it free?
Yes. The blueprints are free and the service is open source. Connect it as a custom connector in Muse and set up your house in minutes.
