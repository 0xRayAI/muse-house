Voice Pack

Six voices for the morning briefing. Users hear each one, pick a favorite, and the 7am briefing switches.

The 6 Voices

VoiceStyle
NewscasterCrisp headline cadence, neutral, facts first
WarmFriendly, conversational, encouraging
BrightUpbeat, optimistic, exclamation-friendly
CalmSlow, steady, reassuring
BoldDirect, punchy, no fluff
GentleSoft, kind, unhurried

Selection Flow

  1. Bling room sends all 6 audio samples as chat attachments (play buttons).
  2. User listens in the room — no website.
  3. User says "I want the [voice] voice".
  4. Room updates the muse-house-morning-briefing cron: changes the VOICE: line to the picked voice and its instruction.
  5. Confirm: "Your briefing now speaks in {voice}. It'll sound like that tomorrow at 7am."

Audio Samples

Samples use generic text (no names, no dollar amounts — PII rule). Hosted at /bling/delights/voice-pack/voice-{id}.mp3.

Persistence

The voice lives in the cron body itself — it stays until the user picks another. No settings page needed.
