Terms of Service

Simple terms for a simple service.

Last updated: October 5, 2026

The service

Muse House provides household blueprints and advisory checks through an MCP API and a website configurator. It is a stateless reference service: it does not move money, send messages, book travel, or take any action in the world on your behalf. Your Muse assistant acts; we only advise.

Advisory, not authority

The Codex check (codex_check) is advisory, not a gate. It flags actions that deserve a second look; your assistant — and you — make the final call. Do not treat a PASS as permission or a FLAG as a prohibition.

Acceptable use

Use the service for its purpose: organizing your household through Muse. Don't abuse the API, don't misrepresent the service as affiliated with Meta, and don't use it to harm others.

Bling shop & payments

The Bling shop sells optional digital extras (trinkets, gizmos, delights) for your house via website Stripe Checkout only. Prices are in US dollars. Cards stay on Stripe — we never see or store card numbers. Bling is not an MCP money-movement tool: no transfers, trades, or payments go through the MCP API. There are no Meta money tools here. Digital goods are delivered to the email you use at checkout. If something doesn't land, write to support and we'll make it right, including refunds where appropriate.

Availability

Provided as-is, without warranties. We aim for reliable uptime but make no guarantees; the blueprints are also open source so you can self-host.

Changes

We may update these terms; continued use after changes means you accept them. Material changes will be noted in the GitHub repository.

Security incidents

Report suspected security incidents affecting Muse House to support@mymuse.house (and open a private GitHub security advisory when appropriate). We aim to acknowledge within 48 hours. For connector-directory or platform-vendor incidents that involve Meta's systems, you may also report to vendor-incident@meta.com.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/0xRayAI/muse-house, use the help form, or email support@mymuse.house.
